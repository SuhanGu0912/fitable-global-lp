FAQ

Common questions

Q. Do I need to drill into the wall to install this?
A. No. Turn the center dial to extend both sides and clamp the bar into the door frame. It takes about 30 seconds and leaves no holes.
Q. Will it fit my door frame? How do I measure?
A. Measure the inner width of your door frame from side to side, excluding the trim. It fits openings 28–38 in (72–96 cm) wide. The flat surface inside the frame must be at least 2.4 in (6.2 cm) deep. Do not install the bar on a shallower surface.
Q. I’m renting. Will this damage the door frame?
A. Nothing gets drilled and nothing gets screwed in. The clamp pads are rubber, not bare metal, and they spread the pressure across a flat part of the frame instead of biting into one point. It also comes down in about 30 seconds, so you can take it off the wall before a walkthrough. Do not install it on a film-wrapped (vinyl-laminated) frame — that surface is too slick to clamp safely.
Q. Will it fit a standard American doorway?
A. Most interior doorways in U.S. homes fall inside the range this bar covers. Measure the inside of the frame with the trim excluded — the bar fits 28–38 in (72–96 cm). Measure before you order, because the trim can make a doorway look wider than the opening actually is.
Q. My door frame has thick trim around it. Does that matter?
A. What matters is the flat surface inside the frame, not the trim around the outside. That flat area needs to be at least 2.4 in (6.2 cm) deep so the clamp has something to sit against.
Q. Can I leave it up all the time?
A. Yes, it is built to stay installed. Turning the center dial the other way takes it down in about 30 seconds when you want the doorway clear.
Q. When does it ship?
A. The U.S. launch is planned for early October. Once orders open we ship right away — you are not waiting on a production run. Waitlist members get the exact date by email first.
Q. What does shipping cost?
A. Shipping and any applicable fees are shown before you pay, with nothing added afterward. Waitlist members get the final numbers by email before orders open.
Q. Can I use resistance bands with it?
A. Yes. Both ends have dedicated band mounts, set apart from the center dial, so a band never pulls on the length adjustment.
Q. How is this different from a standard pull-up bar?
A. The grip extends up to 33 in. The comparison bar shown on this page has a grip of roughly 23.5 in. Both sides expand together, so it’s easier to get level. In destructive static testing it held 1,500 lb.
Q. Can I do more than pull-ups on it?
A. Yes — hanging leg raises and dead-hang stretches both work well. Do not use this bar for dips (pressing up on top of the bar).
Q. Will it work on any door frame material?
A. It installs on solid wood, metal, and PVC/plastic frames with a flat inner surface. Do not install it on film-laminated (vinyl-wrapped) frames, where the surface is too slick for a secure clamp, or on bare concrete, drywall, plywood, or thin paneling.
Q. Is there a warranty?
A. A 1-year warranty is planned, covering manufacturing defects in the structure, lock, or level. Complete coverage, exclusions, and claim instructions will be published before orders open.
